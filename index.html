Closer to what actually happens.

In a world where AI can generate convincing images and text, I want to bring people closer to what actually happens. I visit the places, meet the people and learn how they work. Those encounters become stories that help customers understand your business and what it has to offer.

What would you like people to see?

We start with what you want to communicate, who you want to reach and where the material will be used.

People and their work

Show the people, knowledge and working practices behind your business. Films and photographs that give customers an insight into what you do.

Customer experiences

Tell the story of an actual project through the people involved. We explore the challenge, how the work was done and what came from it.

Places and experiences

Capture the setting, people and moments that define an experience, helping viewers get a better sense of what awaits them.

One project can produce a film, photographs, short videos and written material for your website, social channels or presentations. We agree on the scope together.

I'm Stefán Drengsson.

I'm a filmmaker, photographer and journalist with experience in investigative reporting and communications for businesses. What interests me is people: how they live, work and respond to challenges. I make time to listen, ask questions and give people room to tell their stories.

My background spans investigative journalism at RÚV and Kveikur — including reporting connected to the Samherji case and the book "Nothing to Hide" — to digital communications and content production for large companies. That mix of journalism and visual storytelling shapes how I approach every project.

I also take on website design and digital communications work for select clients, when it's the right fit.
